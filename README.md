# Gilded Midnight — A Heist Game

You are Rook, a thief with champagne taste and a quiet pair of shoes, working three galleries in a single night: the Aurum Gallery, the Sapphire Casino, and the Obsidian Bank. Each night is a top-down stealth puzzle — cross rooms patrolled by guards with visible vision cones, time the sweeps of wall-mounted cameras, sneak past in the dark or toss a decoy coin to pull a guard off his route, and hold E at a CAM panel to kill the cameras for a few precious seconds at the cost of suspicion. Grab the great jewel (and the bonus loot if you are greedy), then slip out the green exit before the alarm meter hits 100%. Getting spotted raises the alarm, a full alarm or a guard's hands end the night, and every night adds more guards, more cameras, and tighter corridors.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD or arrow keys to move, Shift to sneak, click to toss a decoy coin, hold E at a CAM panel to disable the cameras, Enter to start, R to retry.
